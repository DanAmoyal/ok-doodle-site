Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

OK Doodle uploader (Google Drive)

  • Used only by the OK Doodle developer to upload test builds to their own Google Drive.
  • Requests only the drive.file permission: it can see and change files it created, and nothing else in your Drive.
  • Collects no personal data, shares nothing with anyone, and stores its sign-in token only on the developer's computer.
  • You can remove its access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The OK Doodle game (test version)

  • Your account stores a username, an email address and your password (hashed, never readable).
  • Games store your drawings, guesses, coins and streaks, plus your buddy list and players you block.
  • Drawings and guesses are visible only to the two players in that game.
  • Data is held by our database host, Supabase, in the EU. It isn't sold or shared for advertising.
  • To delete your account and its data, ask the developer.

This policy will be updated before the game is released in app stores.
